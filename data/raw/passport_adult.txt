SOURCE_TITLE: Apply for a Dutch passport for an adult
SOURCE_URL: https://www.denhaag.nl/en/passport-and-identity-card/apply-for-a-dutch-passport-for-an-adult/
CATEGORY: Passport and identity card

You do not yet have a Dutch passport, or it will soon expire, and you are 18 years or older? Apply for a passport for adults.

How it works:
1. Read the conditions to confirm you can apply.
2. Make an appointment. You must visit the municipality in person to apply for a passport.
3. Make sure you have everything you need for the appointment.
4. Go to the appointment. The municipality checks your documents and submits your application.
5. Pick up your passport. It will be ready after 6 working days. You do not need an appointment to collect it.

Conditions:
- You have Dutch citizenship.
- You are registered in the Municipality of The Hague.
- You are 18 years or older.
(Different pages apply for a child's passport, Dutch citizens abroad, citizens without a fixed address, or a lost/stolen passport.)

Needed at the appointment:
- All your current passports and identity cards, even if expired or foreign.
- 1 new colour passport photo meeting all requirements, no more than 6 months old.
- A bank card to pay at the counter (cash also accepted at City Hall Spui; credit cards are not accepted).
- Tip: your fingerprints will be taken, so make sure your hands are clean. If you or your companion have trouble with Dutch or English, bring someone who can translate.

Make an appointment:
Book online. If your passport was lost or stolen, first report the missing document before applying for a new one.
Need it quickly? Indicate this during your appointment. If you apply before 14:00, you can collect the passport 2 working days later after 12:00 (extra fee applies).

Fees:
- Regular: €88.65
- Expedited: €148.95 (€60.30 extra)
Ready after 6 working days at the city office where you applied.

Collecting the passport:
- Pick it up yourself; nobody else may collect it for you, and no appointment is needed.
- Bring your pick-up receipt and current identity document.
- You have 3 months to collect it; after that it is destroyed and you must apply (and pay) again.
- Tip: Wednesday mornings are usually quieter. Busy times: 12:00-13:00, Thursday evenings and Friday afternoons at City Hall Spui and the Leyweg City Office.

Frequently asked questions:
- How long is my passport valid? 10 years if you are 18+ when you apply. You may renew at any time, even before expiry.
- Can my partner's name be added? Yes, in 4 prefixed forms (e/v, p/v, g/v, w/v) if married or in a civil partnership.
- What do I do with my old passport? You may never throw away or destroy a passport; you must turn in an expired one, though the municipality can invalidate it at your request so you can keep it.
- What about the passport of someone who has died? It must be turned in at the municipality (City Hall Spui or Leyweg City Office).
- Can I make one appointment for my whole family? Yes, indicate the number of passports needed when booking.
- Business passport (66 pages instead of 34)? Apply the same way as a regular passport; costs the same; you cannot hold both a business and a regular passport.
