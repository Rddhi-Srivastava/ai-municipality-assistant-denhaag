SOURCE_TITLE: Arrange collection of bulky waste or garden waste
SOURCE_URL: https://www.denhaag.nl/en/waste-and-recycling/arrange-collection-of-bulky-waste-and-garden-waste/
CATEGORY: Waste and recycling

Do you have bulky waste or garden waste that you cannot bring to a waste depot yourself? The municipality can collect it for free. If items are still usable or easy to repair, consider a charity shop (kringloopwinkel) or repair café instead, as many charity shops collect for free too.

Conditions:
- The waste does not fit in a container, mini container (kliko) or garbage bag.
- You cannot bring the waste to a waste depot yourself.
- You are not bringing waste on behalf of a company (see waste removal for companies instead).
- The waste is not construction or demolition waste.

How it works:
1. Check the type of waste to confirm the municipality collects it.
2. Make an appointment online; you choose the collection day yourself.
3. Put your waste out according to the rules.

The municipality WILL collect: mattresses; furniture (cupboard, sofa, chair); metal items (old bicycle, drying rack); household appliances (washing machine, dryer, refrigerator, freezer); electric appliances (CD player, TV, speakers); strollers; plastic toys; large garden waste (branches, wood, tree trunk).

The municipality will NOT collect: railway sleepers; gas canisters and fire extinguishers; turf; earth, soil and sand; domestic chemical waste (KCA); construction and demolition waste (rubble, plasterboard, old sanitary equipment, scrap wood, asbestos). These must be brought to a waste depot yourself.

Items that go in collection containers instead (not bulky waste collection): bread, glass, paper and carton, textiles/clothing/shoes, organic waste (GFT), plastic/metal/drink cartons (PMD), frying oil and grease.

Putting out your waste:
- Before 7:45 on the collection day, or after 22:00 the evening before.
- Not next to rubbish containers.
- Bulky waste may not be longer than 1.5 metres, or wider/higher than 0.5 metres.
- Group the same types of waste together (e.g. all metal items together).
- Putting waste out incorrectly, or leaving rubbish outside, can result in a fine.

Fees: Free.

Frequently asked questions:
- Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment? Yes, until 23:59 the day before collection, via the link in your confirmation email or by phone (14070, Mon-Fri 8:30-17:00).
- My bulky waste has not been collected, what do I do? If it is still not collected after 17:00 on the day, report it via the "Report rubbish on the street" page or phone 14070. You do not need to bring it back inside.
- There is bulky waste on the street from someone else, can the municipality collect it? Report it via the "Report rubbish on the street" page.
