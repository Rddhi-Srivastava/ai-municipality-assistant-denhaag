SOURCE_TITLE: Report your move
SOURCE_URL: https://www.denhaag.nl/en/moving-and-immigration/report-your-move/
CATEGORY: Moving and immigration

Are you going to move within The Hague or to The Hague? Report your change of address to the municipality on time. Do this within 5 days after the move.

How it works:
1. Report your move. The fastest way is online. If your family is moving with you, you can report this right away.
2. The municipality will register you at your new address. You will automatically be deregistered from your old address.
3. You will be notified once your address has been changed. The municipality will send a letter to your new address. This can take a month. You can check on MijnOverheid whether your move has been processed.

Conditions:
- You are moving within The Hague, or moving to The Hague from another municipality in the Netherlands.
- You are 16 years or older.
- You can be registered at only 1 address.

When to report the move:
- Before you move: possible as of 4 weeks before your moving date.
- After you have moved: possible up to 5 days after your moving date.
The moving date is the date you specify when you report the move.

Who can report the move:
You can report the move for yourself and for family members moving with you to the same new address (partner if married/registered partnership, children, parents). Children under 16 moving alone must be reported by post or at a municipal counter by their parent/guardian. From age 16 a child can report their own move online with DigiD. You can report a move for someone else only if they authorise you in writing, and only by post or at a municipal counter.

Needed to report the move:
- A copy of your rental contract or purchase deed, showing your name, new address, contract start date and signature (or see the "Proof of occupancy" page if you don't have one).
- If moving in with someone else: written permission from the main occupant, plus a copy of their identity document.
- If reporting by post: a copy of your identity document.

How to report the move:
- Online, using DigiD (only if you are moving yourself). No DigiD? Apply for one first.
- By post: send the moving form to Gemeente Den Haag, DPZ/KCC Unit Beheer en Verstrekkingen, Afdeling Adreswijziging, Postbus 12620, 2500 DL Den Haag.
- At a municipal counter, by appointment (call 14070), only in special circumstances such as a child under 16 moving alone, an authorised report for someone else, or inability to report by post/online.

How long it takes:
- Online: processed within 5 working days, registered on the moving date you specified.
- By post: processed within 2 weeks.

Frequently asked questions:
- What happens to my parking permit when I move? You must always cancel your parking permit when you move. If moving within The Hague to a paid-parking area, apply for a new permit only after you are registered at the new address.
- When will I be registered at my new address? On the moving date you specify, if reported on time. If you report more than 5 days after moving, the day the municipality receives your change of address counts as your moving date, which may affect benefits.
- How can I check the status of my move? Check MijnOverheid.nl.
- Which organisations automatically get my new address? The Tax Administration (Belastingdienst), pension funds, Sociale Verzekeringsbank (SVB), and health insurance companies.
- How do I report a move to another municipality? Report your move in your new municipality; you do not need to deregister from The Hague yourself.
- Can I register at more than 1 address? No, only 1 address, where you spend most nights.
- I am late reporting my move. What do I do? Report as quickly as possible; the day the municipality receives it counts as your moving date.
